The swirl behind the Swirls theme

The field this theme draws is inspired by, and adapted from, the ColorMix animation of ly, a display manager for the console:

    ly, src/animations/ColorMix.zig
    https://github.com/fairyglade/ly

ly is released under the WTFPL, version 2 (the file license.md in its repository; http://www.wtfpl.net/), which permits any use of it, modified or not, without conditions.

What this theme takes from it is the arithmetic: a coordinate bent three times by sines and cosines of itself, and its length pushed through three bands of colour. The theme works that out in Luau on a coarse grid, and the app colours it (docs/theming-media.md, "Fields").
