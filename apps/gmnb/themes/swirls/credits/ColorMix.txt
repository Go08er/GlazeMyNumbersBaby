The swirl behind this theme

The field this theme draws is the background of s0y b0y, the homepage
of GMNB's owner, and is used here at the owner's wish. Its three colours
(#ffcf29, #0ed6f0, #010609), its panels, its hover and its focus ring are
that page's too.

The page's swirl is in turn adapted from the ColorMix animation of ly, a
display manager for the console:

    ly, src/animations/ColorMix.zig
    https://github.com/fairyglade/ly

What this theme takes from it is the arithmetic: a coordinate bent three
times by sines and cosines of itself, and its length pushed through three
bands of colour. The theme works that out in Luau on a coarse grid, and
the app colours it (docs/theming-media.md, "Fields").
